#  Edits I made

##README contains a detailed layout of my steps with the edits I made individually, but to summarize:

##I spend a good amount of time early on manually editing the beginning stage of the app with the help of chat gpt. I had origianlly wanted the posts to fit in with each other similar to how pinterest works, but the images were spilling off the page and overlapping with each other. The common ground I found was to just make one stack of posts. It is less pretty, but more functional, and I was able to build out more functions from there.
##With the emoji picker, I was generally happy with the way it came together, but had to make additional edits where the user is able to post to make it more intuitive. I wanted the size of the emoji to be larger when it was posted as well, so I adjusted size and layout of the post.
##With the pin function, it was originally positioned on the post where it blocked a good amount of text or photo, so I repositioned the pin to be on the corner of the post so it would be out of the way, but easy for users to see.
##The final individual edits I made were to the post structure. When the photos were displayed horizontally, the sizing of the photos to look better and more aesthetic.
